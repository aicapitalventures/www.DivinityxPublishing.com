## 1. Catalog Governance

1. Every title listed below must appear in the Divinityx Publishing Company master publication catalog unless later removed by an approved catalog amendment.
2. A title may appear on DivinityxPublishing.com without being classified as a Voice to Legacy™ project.
3. Voice to Legacy™ classification must be stated only where confirmed.
4. Retailer links must open in a new tab with safe external-link attributes.
5. Prices, rankings, review counts, and format availability must not be hard-coded as permanent facts. Those items require current verification before display.
6. Screenshots of retailer pages are evidence records, not approved website cover assets.
7. Each publication requires a separate clean front-cover image uploaded to `assets/img/books/<slug>/cover-front.webp` or an approved PNG/JPG equivalent before final visual approval.
8. Back-cover images may be used on a detail page where available, but are not required for every title.
9. No title may be represented by fabricated cover art.
10. The public catalog may use a controlled placeholder until the approved cover is uploaded.

## 5. Asset Intake Requirement

Before final visual approval, upload one clean front-cover file for each title. Preferred source order:

1. Original high-resolution cover export
2. Clean retailer cover image without browser interface
3. High-quality KDP cover image

Do not use full Amazon-page screenshots as public cover art.

Recommended upload naming format:

`<slug>-front-cover.png`

Examples:

- `the-fortune-500-blueprint-front-cover.png`
- `credit-alchemy-front-cover.png`
- `project-ascend-the-dream-code-front-cover.png`
